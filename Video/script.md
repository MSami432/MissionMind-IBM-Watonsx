# MissionMind - Demo Video Script 🎬

**Project:** MissionMind (IBM AI Builders Challenge)
**Duration:** ~4 Minutes
**Presenter:** M Sami

---

## Scene 1: Introduction & The Problem (0:00 - 1:00)
**[Visual: Main terminal/dashboard running the MissionMind prototype]**

**Voiceover:** 
"Hello and welcome to MissionMind, developed for the IBM AI Builders Challenge. In modern space exploration, there is a massive telemetry chasm between heavy data generation and human comprehension. Spacecraft generate thousands of low-level data points per second, and human operators often struggle to decode these raw metrics during critical moments. MissionMind is an explainable AI mission-control platform designed to bridge this exact gap."

## Scene 2: Live Telemetry & The Anomaly (1:00 - 2:00)
**[Visual: Trigger a live anomaly in the simulation (e.g., Voltage Drop or Temperature Spike) showing red alerts]**

**Voiceover:** 
"Here you can see our command-line prototype in action, actively monitoring spacecraft telemetry. Suddenly, an anomaly is triggered—a severe voltage drop and temperature spike in the secondary battery system. Normally, an operator would have to manually cross-reference these raw error codes with technical manuals, wasting precious time. But MissionMind handles this differently."

## Scene 3: IBM Granite Model in Action (2:00 - 3:00)
**[Visual: Display the AI-generated operational briefing on the screen]**

**Voiceover:** 
"Instead of panic, our system instantly routes this low-level telemetry data to the IBM Granite Base model. Optimized for low-latency execution and rapid decode scheduling, the Granite model processes the complex data and immediately generates a plain-language, explainable briefing. It clearly outlines the root cause of the voltage drop and provides a step-by-step strategic recommendation to isolate the affected battery cell."

## Scene 4: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) & Execution (3:00 - 4:00)
**[Visual: Prompt asking for human input (Approve/Modify) and then showing the Decision Log]**

**Voiceover:** 
"However, we believe that AI should assist, not replace, the human operator. That’s why MissionMind is built strictly on a Human-in-the-Loop architecture. The operator reviews the Granite-generated briefing, verifies the logic, and makes the final decision to execute the command. Once approved, the action is securely saved into our Decision Log, ensuring full transparency and accountability for every system change."

## Scene 5: Conclusion (4:00 - 4:30)
**[Visual: Final clean state of the dashboard/terminal and project title]**

**Voiceover:** 
"By empowering operators with transparent, explainable AI, we reduce cognitive load and accelerate critical decision-making. MissionMind is ready to advance space exploration. Thank you for watching."
